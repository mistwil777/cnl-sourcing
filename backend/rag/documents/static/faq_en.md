### What are the customs duties for importing from Vietnam?
Customs duties vary depending on the nature of the product (HS/NC code).
Since the EVFTA free trade agreement (2020), many Vietnamese products
benefit from reduced or zero rates into the EU. The applicable rates
depend on the customs code of your product. CNL Sourcing can guide you
but recommends consulting a freight forwarder or the French customs
services for your specific case.
Source: douane.gouv.fr

### Does the EVFTA agreement apply to my products?
The Free Trade Agreement between the European Union and Vietnam (EVFTA),
in force since August 2020, provides for the progressive elimination of
99% of customs duties. To benefit from preferential rates, your goods
must be accompanied by an EUR.1 certificate of origin or an origin
declaration from an approved Vietnamese supplier.

### What documents are required to import from Vietnam?
Documents typically required for importation from Vietnam:
- Commercial Invoice
- Packing List
- Bill of Lading (sea freight) or Air Waybill (air freight)
- Certificate of Origin (ASEAN Form D or EUR.1 for EVFTA)
- Compliance certificate depending on product type (CE marking, phytosanitary...)
CNL Sourcing assists you in verifying these documents.

## Vietnam — Market and Business Culture

### Which sectors are most developed in Vietnam?
Vietnam is particularly strong in the following sectors:
- Textiles and garments: 2nd largest global exporter of clothing
- Electronics and components: Samsung, Intel have major production there
- Shoes and leather goods: strong artisanal know-how
- Furniture and decoration: wood, rattan, bamboo, lacquerware
- Food: coffee, spices, seafood, rice
- Crafts: ceramics, silk, embroidery, lacquered objects

### Are there specific risks when sourcing from Vietnam?
The main points to watch out for:
- Thorough supplier verification (visits, references)
- Contracts in English and/or Vietnamese with precise clauses
- Limited deposits (30% maximum before production)
- Quality control before shipment is strongly recommended
- Deadlines often longer than announced
Anna knows these on-the-ground realities and guides you to avoid them.

### What are the important cultural differences in Vietnamese business?
Vietnamese business culture values the personal relationship before the
transaction. Trust is built over time. Negotiations are indirect — a
direct refusal is rare. Punctuality is relative. The network (quan hệ)
is paramount: having a trusted intermediary like CNL Sourcing
fundamentally changes the quality of supplier relationships.
